Self-Hosted Architecture Highlights

Everything runs on your machine — no external tracking, no third-party analytics, no remote calls.

Dual Table Normalization

SchemaAdapter automatically discovers and maps non-standard column names across both HiTek and ICMR tables.

Symmetric BFS Traversal

Walks phone → Aadhaar → alternate numbers in both directions, up to 3 hops deep, without missing links.

High-Speed SQLite Tuning

WAL journal mode, 64 MB memory page cache and 2 GB mmap keep indexed lookups under 150 ms.

Zero Data Leakage

Runs 100% locally on your machine or private server. No external tracking, third-party analytics or remote calls.

Production Guardrails

Built-in sliding-window rate limiting per IP, safe SQL identifier quoting and stateful /health monitoring.

One-Click Clipboard Copy

Click any discovered phone number or Aadhaar UID chip to instantly copy it to your system clipboard.

Self-Host Your Identity Engine

Clone the repository, point .env at your local SQLite database, and deploy via systemd or Docker.
